## 🧩 How the simulation models UI/UX improvements

- Core Information: Reordering and highlighting of labels `problem_description`, `priority`, and `status` are performed by moving identified primary fields to the top and marking `ui.highlight = True`.
- Collapsible Sections: Secondary fields (`history`, `logs`, `related_tickets`) are set `ui.collapsed = True` by default.
- Attachment Preview: The transformer sets `ui.preview_mode="side-panel"` to model non-blocking preview vs full overlay.
- Internal vs Customer Inputs: `notes` fields are marked `ui.internal: True` to avoid confusion with `action` fields that may be customer-visible.
- Confirmation Prompts: `action` items with `meta.scope == "ambiguous"` set `ui.require_confirmation = True`.
- Robust Error Handling: Transformer detects `unsafe_html` and invalid field types, sanitizes content or raises errors, and adds warnings.
- Visual Scoring: Simple heuristics quantify improvements like `hierarchy_clarity_score` and `mis_operation_risk` so the comparison is testable and automated.
